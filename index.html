      Sensor data

      How it works
      1. Scan sensor: take a photo of your reader, or a screenshot of your Libre or Dexcom app. The app reads your current sugar and trend arrow and fills them in for you to check and save.
      2. FreeStyle Libre: sign in at libreview.com, open Glucose History and choose Download glucose data.
      3. Dexcom: sign in to Dexcom Clarity, choose Export (top right) and save the CSV.
      4. For a full history, tap Import file and pick the file. Readings, plus any insulin and carbs from the sensor app, are added. Duplicates are skipped.

      Insulin pump

      How it works
      1. Scan pump screen: take a photo or screenshot of your pump's bolus history. The app reads each dose, its time and any carbs, and shows them for you to check before adding.
      3. The app only reads your doses. It never sends anything to your pump. Basal (background) insulin isn't imported.

      Dose settings

      Copy these from your diabetes team's plan. The app won't suggest any dose until you confirm them.

      Many people need more insulin for breakfast because of morning hormones, so their breakfast number is lower. Snacks and corrections use the ratio for the time of day.

      Don't know your ratios?

      Diabetes teams often start from your total daily insulin (all rapid plus long-acting in a typical day) using two widely used rules: carb ratio ≈ 500 ÷ total daily dose, and correction factor ≈ 100 ÷ total daily dose (1800 ÷ total daily dose in mg/dL). These are only starting points. Check them with your diabetes team before you rely on them.

      About AI: meal, snack and hypo suggestions and the review on Insights are made by AI (Claude) and are labelled ✦ AI. AI can make mistakes, so check anything that looks wrong. Insulin doses are never set by AI. They're calculated from your diabetes team's numbers below. If AI isn't available, the app falls back to its built-in suggestions.

      T1Dy is a prototype and not a medical device. Suggestions follow general UK guidance and your own team's numbers. Always follow your diabetes team's advice. For a severe hypo, or if someone can't swallow or is unconscious, call 999. Your data is stored only in this browser.
